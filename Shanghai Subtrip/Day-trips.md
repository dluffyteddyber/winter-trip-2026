# Trips around Shanghai (周边遊)

The main itinerary leaves room for **one day trip on Tue 8 Dec**, the last full day with the family. If you choose **Stay in Shanghai** after the family leaves, you get **9–12 Dec** as well.

Travel times are typical ranges by high-speed train from Shanghai, not December timetables.

| Destination | From Shanghai | Day trip or overnight? | Why go | Winter note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Suzhou 苏州** | ~30 min | Day trip | Classical gardens, canals, Pingjiang Road, Suzhou Museum, Suzhou noodles | Recommended for 8 Dec with the family. Go early to beat tour groups. |
| **Zhujiajiao 朱家角** | ~1 h by Metro Line 17 | Half or full day | The closest water town. No train ticket needed. | A lighter option for 8 Dec if 姨媽 is still tired from the race. |
| **Hangzhou 杭州** | ~45–60 min | Best with 1 night | West Lake, Lingyin Temple, Longjing tea villages | In the plan for 9–10 Dec if you stay in Shanghai. |
| **Nanjing 南京** | ~1–1.5 h | Day trip or 1 night | Ming city wall, Purple Mountain 紫金山, Nanjing Museum, duck dishes | An option for 11 Dec if you stay in Shanghai. |
| **Wuzhen 乌镇 / Xitang 西塘** | ~1.5–2 h (train + taxi or bus) | 1 night for Wuzhen's evening lights | Canal water towns | Weekdays are much quieter. |

Huangshan has been dropped. With 9–12 Dec now going to Wuhan by default, there's no room for a two-night mountain trip.

## Wuhan 武汉, if you go with the family (9–12 Dec)

This isn't a day trip, but it's the default plan for these days on the main page.

- **Yellow Crane Tower 黄鹤楼** and a walk across the **Yangtze River Bridge 长江大桥**.
- **Hubei Provincial Museum 湖北省博物馆**, home of the Marquis Yi bronze bells 曾侯乙编钟.
- **East Lake 东湖** greenway on foot or by bike.
- **Tanhualin 昙华林** old lanes and **Hubu Alley 户部巷** snacks.
- **Food:** 过早 breakfast culture. Try 热干面 hot dry noodles, 豆皮, 面窝, 排骨藕汤 and 武昌鱼.
