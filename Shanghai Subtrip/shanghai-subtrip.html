        Good to know

        • Late arrival: CZ3596 lands at Hongqiao 虹桥 at 22:10. Plan on a taxi or DiDi to wherever you're staying, and nothing else that night.
        • Weather: Shanghai is about 4–12 °C in early December, damp and often grey. Wuhan is similar or a little colder. Older buildings are poorly heated, so pack layers and a waterproof, plus something warm for standing out at the race.
        • Internet: Google and WhatsApp are blocked on the mainland. Set up a roaming eSIM or VPN before 12.5.
        • Paying: link Alipay or WeChat Pay to a foreign card, and add DiDi for taxis. Carry a little cash.
        • Trains: book on 12306 or Trip.com. Your passport is the ticket, and sales open about 15 days ahead. The Wuhan → Hong Kong train clears immigration at West Kowloon 西九龍.
