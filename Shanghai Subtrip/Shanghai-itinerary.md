## Getting around

- **Hongqiao 虹桥** is both your arrival airport and the station for the Suzhou, Hangzhou, Nanjing and Wuhan trains.
- **Trains:** book through China Railway 12306 or Trip.com. Your passport is your ticket, and sales normally open about 15 days ahead.
- **In the city:** use the metro plus DiDi 滴滴. Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay with your foreign card before you fly.
- **Internet:** set up a roaming eSIM or VPN before 5 Dec. Google and WhatsApp are blocked on the mainland.

## Weather and packing

Early December in Shanghai is roughly **4–12 °C**, damp and often grey. Wuhan is similar or a little colder. Older buildings have weak heating. Bring warm layers, a waterproof, comfortable walking shoes, and something warm for standing outside at the race.
