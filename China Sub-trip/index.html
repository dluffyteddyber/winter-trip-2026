FRI / DEC25Day 01 · Kunming

Arrive. Meet. Eat something good.

Arrival day · overnight Kunming · night 1 of 9
  1. ARRIVE
    Meet at the hotel, not at the airport

    Let each departure group travel into town when they land. Share the hotel’s Chinese address and meeting time; avoid one group waiting hours in arrivals.

  2. 15:00
    Check in around Green Lake or the old streets

    A central location makes tomorrow’s food day easier. If flights arrive late, drop the afternoon walk entirely.

  3. 18:30
    A gentle first dinner

    Crossing-the-bridge noodles, shared vegetables and tea. Walk around Kunming Old Street only if everyone has energy.

Airport transfer allowance: roughly 60–90 minutes after leaving arrivals, depending on traffic and hotel. This is a planning buffer, not a quoted journey time.

Stay: central Kunming · 2-night bookingNoodle guide ↗
SAT / DEC26Day 02 · Kunming

The market is the main event.

Zhuanxin → Green Lake → old streets · night 2 of 9
  1. 08:30
    Zhuanxin Market 篆新农贸市场

    Allow about two hours to graze. Share tofu-pudding noodles, rice-cake snacks and something sweet. Buy one portion at a time so you can try more stalls.

  2. 11:30
    A proper noodle lunch

    Choose small-pot noodles if breakfast was light, or a smaller lunch if the market won. Ask for chilli separately where possible.

  3. 13:30
    Green Lake 翠湖 & Wenlin Street

    Take a taxi between areas if useful. Walk the lake, stop for Yunnan coffee or tea, and leave a break at the hotel.

  4. 18:30
    Shared Yunnan dinner

    Make this the first longer meal together. Order a mix of meat, vegetables and a local speciality, rather than four separate mains.

Main food day · no long transferMarket & snack guide ↗Market map ↗
SUN / DEC27Day 03 · Kunming → Dali

Trade the city for a courtyard.

Rail transfer + Dali Old Town · night 3 of 9
  1. 08:00
    Breakfast, checkout & station transfer

    Choose the train and departure station together: Kunming and Kunming South are different stations. Aim to be at the correct station 45–60 minutes before departure.

  2. 10:00*
    Train to Dali 大理站

    Target a mid-morning service; rail time is around 2–3 hours. No train number or departure time is booked.

  3. 14:00
    Lunch and check-in near Dali Old Town

    Dali station is in the newer city, not the old town. Reserve 45–75 minutes for the onward transfer and check-in, adjusting for traffic.

  4. 16:00
    Old-town lanes, then a Bai-style dinner

    Walk Renmin Road and nearby side lanes. Try grilled milk cheese as a snack and have dinner close to your hotel.

*Target window only. Treat this as a half-day move from hotel to hotel, even though the train itself is shorter.

Stay: Dali Old Town · 3-night bookingRail reference ↗
MON / DEC28Day 04 · Xizhou & Zhoucheng

A market morning. Indigo afternoon.

Village day · optional craft workshop · night 4 of 9
  1. 08:15
    Leave for Xizhou 喜洲

    Get a driver quote for the four of you, or compare taxis. Keep today on the north-west side of Erhai rather than attempting the entire lake circuit.

  2. 09:00
    Market breakfast & Bai courtyards

    Find freshly made Xizhou baba: share a savoury and a sweet version. Spend the rest of the morning exploring the village’s architecture and side streets.

  3. 12:30
    Lunch in Xizhou

    Keep this a leisurely meal, then decide whether the group wants a craft session or more village wandering.

  4. 14:00
    Optional tie-dye session in Zhoucheng 周城

    Allow roughly 1½–2½ hours including the introduction and making. Confirm workshop availability, price and drying/collection arrangements directly.

  5. 17:00
    Back to Dali for a quiet evening

    If a workshop runs long, drop extra stops. This is a village day with time to linger.

TUE / DEC29Day 05 · Erhai Lake

One stretch of lakeshore. No hurry.

Planned leave · easy walking / cycling · night 5 of 9
  1. 09:00
    Late breakfast in the old town

    A slower start after yesterday’s outing. Buy water and a snack before leaving for the lake.

  2. 10:30
    Longkan or Caicun lakeside 龙龛 / 才村

    Pick one west-shore area. Walk for an hour or two, or hire bicycles where permitted. Confirm local access and return arrangements on the day.

  3. 13:00
    A long lunch & lakeside tea

    This is the day for a good conversation and a café with a view. Winter wind can make the water’s edge chilly even when the sun is out.

  4. 16:00
    Back to town; free time before dinner

    If the lake is windy or wet, substitute the Three Pagodas area or a courtyard café. Pack tonight for tomorrow’s train.

WED / DEC30Day 06 · Dali → Lijiang

North to the Naxi old town.

Planned leave · rail transfer · night 6 of 9
  1. 08:00
    Checkout & transfer to Dali station

    Have breakfast early. Allow for the drive from the old town and the station entry buffer.

  2. 10:00*
    Train to Lijiang 丽江站

    Aim for a mid-morning service; allow about 1½–2½ hours on the train. Confirm the actual schedule when tickets are released.

  3. 14:00
    Lunch and a warm room

    Reserve roughly 30–60 minutes for the station-to-hotel transfer and luggage drop-off. Ask the hotel where the car can stop; old-town lanes may require a short walk with bags.

  4. 16:00
    First wander through Dayan Old Town

    Keep this an easy introduction to the lanes and waterways. Save any mountain outing for a later day.

  5. 18:30
    Cured-pork-rib hotpot 腊排骨火锅

    A warming group dinner, with vegetables and local sides. Choose a quieter lane for your hotel rather than the busiest bar street.

THU / DEC31Day 07 · Baisha & New Year’s Eve

Mountain views, then a table for four.

Planned leave · village outing · night 7 of 9
  1. 09:00
    Breakfast & a taxi to Baisha 白沙

    Keep the morning unhurried. This is a village outing rather than a high-altitude mountain ascent.

  2. 10:00
    Village lanes and a mountain-facing café

    Walk first, then settle somewhere with a view if the weather is clear. Murals or a local craft stop can be added after checking access and opening arrangements.

  3. 12:30
    Lunch, then back to rest

    Give everyone time to warm up and recharge before dinner. If mountain tickets or weather favour today, swap this outing with tomorrow.

  4. 18:30
    Pre-book a New Year’s Eve dinner

    Agree on budget, dietary requirements and any set menu in advance. Choose the atmosphere you actually want: a relaxed meal, music, or an early night.

No public countdown or fireworks programme has been verified. Tomorrow’s early mountain start is optional; tonight need not revolve around midnight.

FRI / JAN01Day 08 · Choose your mountain day

Snow peaks, or another slow morning.

Optional full-day excursion · night 8 of 9
  1. 07:00*
    Optional: Jade Dragon Snow Mountain 玉龙雪山

    Have an early breakfast, then use a pre-arranged driver or a clearly itemised tour. Departure depends on your actual entry and cable-car slots.

  2. MORNING
    Choose the experience before buying

    Glacier Park’s large cable car reaches about 4,506 m. A valley/meadow visit is a different product, with different tickets and elevations. Check exactly what is included; the high cable car is not compulsory.

  3. 12:30
    Lunch & Blue Moon Valley 蓝月谷

    Only combine the valley with the mountain if the booked slots leave enough time. Avoid adding every cable car or a show to the same day.

  4. 16:30
    Return to Lijiang and keep dinner simple

    Allow extra time for queues, wind-related changes and the drive back. Pack for Kunming tomorrow.

Town-day alternative: breakfast at 09:30 → Shuhe 束河 lanes and lunch → tea or a craft stop → early dinner. Choose this if the group wants rest, mountain tickets are unavailable, or conditions are unsuitable.

The high cable car is a significant altitude change. Do not continue higher with symptoms of altitude illness; worsening symptoms require descent. Review CDC altitude guidance before choosing. A valley or town day remains available.
