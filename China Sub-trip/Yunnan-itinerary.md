## Food priorities

**Kunming:** try crossing-the-bridge noodles **过桥米线**, small-pot noodles **小锅米线**, and tofu-pudding noodles **豆花米线**. Zhuanxin Market is a useful place for a shared snack crawl. [Yunnan tourism noodle guide](https://www.visityunnanchina.com/experiences/food/summary-kunming-rice-noodles), [Kunming's market guide](https://m.kunming.cn/en/c/2023-08-31/13751486.shtml).

**Dali/Xizhou:** build a breakfast around **喜洲粑粑**, the local flatbread. Add grilled milk cheese **烤乳扇** and a shared Bai meal. Xizhou's Linden Centre includes market visits and local bread-making in its own village programme. [Linden Centre programme](https://www.linden-centre.com/uploadfile/2025/0525/20250525132439149.pdf), [Kunming market guide on grilled milk fans](https://m.kunming.cn/en/c/2023-08-31/13751486.shtml).

**Lijiang:** look for cured-pork-rib hotpot **腊排骨火锅**, chickpea jelly **鸡豆凉粉**, and local baba bread. Restaurant selection still needs current reviews, opening checks and the group's dietary preferences. [Research overview of Naxi food](https://www.atlantis-press.com/article/125989230.pdf).

**Seasonal expectation:** December is outside the main fresh wild-mushroom season. Mushroom dishes may be available, but fresh seasonal varieties should not be the basis for choosing the trip. [Yunnan tourism's seasonal food overview](https://www.visityunnanchina.com/plan-your-trip/travel-blog/yunnan-many-splendoured-life05).

## Staying and getting around

- In Kunming, compare central hotels around Green Lake or the old streets for the first stay; select the final hotel with the actual departure time in mind.
- In Dali, start with hotels near the old town for easy meals and organise Erhai/Xizhou outings from that base.
- In Lijiang, look for a quiet room with heating explicitly confirmed and a practical luggage drop-off point. Winter is cold, as the [Yunnan tourism guide](https://www.visityunnanchina.com/culture/ethnic-villages-ancient-towns/old-town-lijiang) notes.
- Use rail between the three cities. Working allowances are around 2–3 hours Kunming–Dali, 1.5–2.5 hours Dali–Lijiang and 3.5–5 hours Lijiang–Kunming, plus local transfers and station time. These are indicative ranges, not December timetable confirmations. [Rail route reference](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/china-trains/kunming-lijiang.htm).
- Compare taxis with a quoted driver for village/lake outings; four people can share the vehicle cost. No car or driver price is confirmed.
