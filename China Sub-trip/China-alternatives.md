# Other China routes for 25 December–3 January

These are alternatives to the Yunnan segment within the longer Asia trip. Each uses **nine nights**. Rankings are planning judgement for this group. Flights from Hong Kong and Bangkok, onward flights on 3 January and date-specific hotel prices still need checking.

## 1. Chengdu + Chongqing: strongest alternative for food and city life

**Suggested split: Chengdu 25–28 December (4 nights), Chongqing 29 December–2 January (5 nights). Depart 3 January.**

Choose this if the group wants to organise each day around eating, neighbourhood walks and city sights. Chengdu is a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy, recognised for Sichuan cooking and its range of flavours. [UNESCO: Chengdu](https://www.unesco.org/en/creative-cities/chengdu).

- **Chengdu:** teahouses, a panda visit if everyone is interested, Sichuan dishes, noodles and a relaxed neighbourhood day.
- **Chongqing:** river views, layered streets, hotpot and a mix of daytime walks and evening skyline outings.
- **Food shortlist to investigate:** dan dan noodles, mapo tofu, wontons, Sichuan dishes to share, Chongqing xiaomian and hotpot. Agree on spice levels and use a split hotpot broth if helpful; do not infer anyone's tolerance from nationality.
- **Logistics:** compare flights into Chengdu and out of Chongqing, including whether Chengdu services use TFU or CTU. Allow a substantial part of 29 December for the hotel-to-hotel train move. Current route references show a range of roughly 1–3 hours on the train, depending on service and station; the holiday timetable is unverified. [Rail reference](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/china-trains/high-speed/chongqing-chengdu.htm).
- **Winter compromise:** expect cool, often cloudy or foggy weather rather than dependable blue skies. Chengdu's January climate averages are about 2.4°C minimum and 9.5°C maximum; these are historical averages, not a forecast. [WMO/CMA climate data](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=547), [Chongqing climate guide](https://welcomecq.cn/plan/climate-and-weather/).

**My take:** put this alongside Yunnan in the group's first vote. Its two-base structure suits people who want time to eat and explore rather than repack every few nights.

## 2. Xiamen + Quanzhou: coastal walks, heritage and Fujian cooking

**Suggested split: Quanzhou 25–28 December (4 nights), Xiamen 29 December–2 January (5 nights). Depart 3 January.** Reverse the order if the flight times work better.

Choose this for seafood, snack-hopping, temples and historic streets, with a coastal city base. Quanzhou joined UNESCO's Creative Cities Network for gastronomy in 2025, and its cuisine reflects maritime trading connections. [UNESCO: Quanzhou](https://www.unesco.org/en/creative-cities/quanzhou).

- **Quanzhou:** make time for old-town walks and meals rather than treating it as a quick excursion.
- **Xiamen:** coastal walks, cafés and a possible Gulangyu visit after checking ferry arrangements.
- **Food shortlist:** ginger duck **姜母鸭**, noodle soup **面线糊**, spring rolls **润饼**, oyster omelette **海蛎煎** and peanut soup **花生汤**. [Quanzhou city food overview](https://www.quanzhou.gov.cn/gastronomy/en/Updates/news/202512/t20251218_3243157.htm), [Fujian food guide](https://www.gofujian.com.cn/quanzhou/food.html).
- **Logistics:** compare Xiamen XMN and Quanzhou Jinjiang JJN as gateways, and use rail for the city-to-city move once station and hotel locations are chosen. Direct international services for the exact dates are not established here.
- **Winter compromise:** this is a coastal walking trip, not a promise of swimming weather. Bring layers for sea wind. Consult the [WMO/CMA Xiamen climate page](https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=563) when comparing winter conditions.

**My take:** the best option here if the group wants a food-and-heritage trip with little interest in snow or high mountains. Check whether everyone wants a full ten days in two relatively relaxed bases.

## 3. Guangzhou + Shunde: a Cantonese food trip

**Suggested split: Guangzhou 25 December (1 night), Shunde 26–28 December (3 nights), Guangzhou 29 December–2 January (5 nights). Depart 3 January.** The first Guangzhou night allows the two departure groups to arrive without an immediate intercity transfer.

Choose this if meals are the main event and Hong Kong is a convenient next stop in your longer trip. Shunde is a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy and a centre of Cantonese culinary traditions. [UNESCO: Shunde](https://www.unesco.org/en/creative-cities/shunde).

- **Guangzhou:** dim sum, roast meats, claypot rice, old neighbourhoods and relaxed meal-focused days.
- **Shunde:** build the stay around Cantonese meals and milk desserts, with restaurant choices checked closer to booking.
- **Logistics:** compare meeting in Guangzhou CAN against entering through Hong Kong and travelling onward. Hong Kong–mainland travel still involves the relevant border/document checks. Select rail stations and hotels together before comparing journey times.
- **Main compromise:** ask your Hong Kong friend how much of Guangdong they have already explored. Familiarity could affect its appeal as the special group segment of a longer trip.

**My take:** a strong food option, especially if you choose Hong Kong as your January base. It ranks below Yunnan and Chengdu–Chongqing for this initial shortlist because the group may want more varied scenery or somewhere less familiar.

## How I would decide

Send the group three choices first: **Yunnan**, **Chengdu–Chongqing**, and **Xiamen–Quanzhou**. Ask each person for their first choice, a food budget, previous visits and one must-do activity. Compare international flight options only for the top two, then choose the route.

Avoid combining Yunnan with Chengdu–Chongqing in these ten days. Each can fill this segment comfortably on its own.
