## My recommendation

**Yunnan is now the chosen option.** The other China routes below are retained as background alternatives.

These rankings are my judgement for this group, rather than a claim that one destination is objectively best. Your Thai friends' interests and everyone's previous China visits could change the ranking; nationality does not establish food or weather preferences.

| Route | Main reason to choose it | Main compromise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kunming–Dali–Lijiang | Food, lake scenery, villages and mountain views in one trip | More hotel changes; cold Lijiang nights |
| Chengdu–Chongqing | Shared meals, Sichuan cooking, pandas, teahouses and a dramatic city skyline | Cool, often grey winter conditions; agree on spice levels |
| Xiamen–Quanzhou | Seafood, Fujian snacks, historic streets and coastal walks | Less of a mountain or snow experience; sea wind can feel cold |
| Guangzhou–Shunde | A trip centred on Cantonese cooking with Hong Kong as a possible onward base | Ask whether this would feel sufficiently new to your Hong Kong friend |

## Before booking

1. Share the Yunnan timeline with the other three and agree on the optional mountain day, food preferences and activity pace.
2. Set a per-person budget excluding flights and confirm room sharing and dietary requirements.
3. Resolve your own 24 December location and where you want to go on 3 January.
4. Compare flights from Hong Kong and Bangkok together, including arrival times and baggage. No direct services or prices for these dates have been verified.
5. Check entry requirements for each person's actual passport or mainland travel document, including the crossing into January 2027. Hong Kong residence alone does not determine the applicable document.

The saved alternatives are background options rather than extra stops to fit into the selected Yunnan route.
